# Heyy Studio production cutover checklist

Use this once, after all product phases and final QA are complete.

## Canonical site URL

Beta:

`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://heyystudiobeta.netlify.app`

Production:

`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://www.heyystudio.com`

All Heyy Studio email CTAs use this central value. Do not hard-code the beta or live hostname into individual templates.

## Customer billing portal

Create a dedicated production Customer Portal configuration and set:

- Payment-method updates: enabled
- Subscription cancellation: enabled as intended by Heyy Studio
- Invoice history: **disabled** (Heyy Studio Payment history is the customer invoice library)
- Plan switching: use the Heyy Studio-controlled plan-change flow rather than an uncontrolled portal switch if applicable

Set its ID as:

`STRIPE_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION_ID=...`

## Heyy Studio invoice identity

Configure these server values before the first real customer payment:

- `HEYY_INVOICE_BUSINESS_NAME`
- `HEYY_INVOICE_ABN`
- `HEYY_INVOICE_ADDRESS`
- `HEYY_INVOICE_EMAIL`
- `HEYY_INVOICE_GST_REGISTERED=true` when appropriate

The PDF invoice sent to customers is generated by Heyy Studio from the successful payment record. It must contain the correct legal supplier details and tax/GST amount actually charged.

## Payment emails

After Heyy Studio payment receipts have been verified in production:

- avoid duplicate automatic payment-success emails from the payment processor
- keep required failure/recovery notifications covered by Heyy Studio
- send subscription, credit-pack and expert-production payment confirmations from Heyy Studio
- attach the Heyy Studio invoice PDF
- keep invoices available at `/account/payments`

## Domain and auth

- Connect `www.heyystudio.com`
- Update Google OAuth allowed origins and redirect URLs
- Update authentication callback URLs
- Verify email links all resolve to `www.heyystudio.com`

## Final repository scan

Search before launch for:

- `localhost`
- `heyystudiobeta`
- test/sandbox price IDs
- test customer email addresses
- customer-facing provider/platform names

## Final smoke test

1. Create a new account and receive the welcome email.
2. Open every CTA in the welcome email and confirm production-domain routing.
3. Buy a credit pack; confirm Heyy Studio payment email + PDF invoice + Payment history.
4. Subscribe to Starter; repeat receipt/invoice test.
5. Upgrade/change plan; confirm billing state.
6. Submit and pay an expert-production quote; confirm receipt/invoice.
7. Test a payment failure and recovery.
8. Cancel a subscription.
9. Delete a disposable account.
